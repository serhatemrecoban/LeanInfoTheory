## 6. Preferred proof route and alternatives — advisory

For Chebyshev, the preferred analytic imports are pinned
`Mathlib.Probability.Moments.Variance` and
`Mathlib.Analysis.SpecificLimits.Basic`, with only actually needed focused
additions. Artifact presence is an environment observation, not a mathematical
contract or a requirement to import unused machinery under an alternative route.
A short private finset event-sum specialization may avoid importing the existing
TotalVariation owner solely for its general conversion theorem. Compare the
actual import cost and proof size; do not duplicate that general theorem publicly.

1. On iid support, expand `finiteProduct_apply`, convert the product to Real,
   use positivity of each supported atom and logarithm-of-product, then the
   existing nonzero branch of `selfInfo`. At zero length the sum is empty.
2. Choose discrete measurable structures locally. `MemLp.of_discrete` supplies
   square-integrability for every finite word function. Transfer coordinate
   independence through measurable `selfInfo p` using `IndepFun.comp`.
3. `finiteProduct_map_eval` and `PMF.toMeasure_map` identify coordinate laws;
   integral and variance map identities transfer the base mean and variance.
   The existing entropy integral theorem identifies the mean with H.
4. Apply `IndepFun.variance_sum` to obtain `n * V_p`; scale by inverse n for
   normalized variance `V_p / n`, explicitly requiring `n > 0` for division.
   Supported block additivity supplies the AE/event-probability substitution.
5. Apply the exact Chebyshev event with epsilon positive. Convert its finite
   ENNReal probability to Real with nonnegativity/finiteness proved. The scalar
   sequence `V_p / ((n : Real) * epsilon^2)` tends to zero; squeeze.
6. Outside support has probability zero. Thus, for positive n, complement
   typical probability equals the deviation probability even though the sets
   differ. Complement subtraction gives probability tending to one.
7. On T, logarithm monotonicity/exponentiation gives both mass bounds.
   Summing the lower atom bound and using total probability at most one gives
   the upper cardinality bound. Summing the upper atom bound gives the actual
   probability-weighted lower cardinality bound. Exponential positivity allows
   division without assuming positive entropy or nonempty typical sets.

Alternative: expand the centered square in a finite sum and use the actual
coordinate pair law to cancel cross terms, then a finite second-moment Markov
estimate. This avoids `Measure.pi` and retains the same finite-PMF endpoint.
It is not authorization to duplicate general probability machinery publicly.
An infinite strong-law construction currently adds work without changing the
required numerical limit; revisit only if a concrete simplification is shown.

The implementation must not promote this outline or an uncompiled draft to
proof-complete evidence. The early C19.01 checkpoint and C19.02 limit remain
required implementation work after approval.

An optional private algebraic characterization can replace the normalized
typicality calculation inside a proof by
`x in q_n.support and abs(L_n(x)-(n : Real)*H) < (n : Real)*epsilon`.
It is equivalent to the public guarded predicate for every natural n: for n=0
the strict inequality is impossible, and for n>0 multiply by n. Preserve the
signed right side; `abs(n*epsilon)` would incorrectly admit negative widths.
This is proof advice only, not an extra public theorem, required helper or change
to the public normalized membership contract.
