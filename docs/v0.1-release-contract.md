## 1. Release meaning and mathematical scope

`v0.1.0` will be a reusable Lean 4/mathlib foundation for finite discrete
information theory over mathlib probability mass functions. It is not a claim
that information theory, Cover--Thomas Chapter 2, or the public API is complete
or permanently frozen.

The supported mathematical scope is:

- finite PMF utilities, support sums, maps, binds, and pure laws;
- finite channels, composition, induced joint laws, and support-aware channel
  identities;
- entropy, conditional entropy, mutual information, conditional mutual
  information, and their finite chain rules;
- finite entropy bounds and equality cases, binary and q-ary entropy, and Fano
  inequalities;
- finite dependent families, including overlapping atoms, family chain rules,
  submodularity, conditioning reduction, n-way subadditivity, and
  entropy-additivity characterizations;
- conditional laws, product and finite-sum identities;
- mathlib KL bridges, with canonical `ENNReal` statements and appropriately
  guarded real-valued forms;
- log-sum, entropy concavity, channel convexity, and common-base conditional KL;
- independence, conditional independence, Markov chains, data processing,
  sufficiency, recovery, and supported equality characterizations.

The release explicitly excludes coding theorems and capacity, AEP and
typicality, stochastic-process and entropy-rate hierarchies, general measurable
entropy or sufficiency, Pinsker/topological/continuity theory, majorization and
Birkhoff theory, canonical or minimal sufficiency, certificate search/import
automation, paper-specific perfect-functional-representation constructions,
and all new Chunk 9 mathematics.

## 2. Certificate ownership

Release-preparation Step 4 moves certificate representation and checking to
downstream ownership; they are not part of the stable LeanInfoTheory `v0.1.0`
mathematical surface. This includes the former generic certificate core,
certificate adapters, checked-certificate demonstrations, and certificate-only
example sections.

The extraction target includes the responsibilities currently represented by:

- `LeanInfoTheory.EntropyExpr`;
- `LeanInfoTheory.EntropyVal`;
- `LeanInfoTheory.PrimitiveIneq`;
- `LeanInfoTheory.Certificate` and `LeanInfoTheory.Certificate.Checked`;
- the certificate demonstration modules under `LeanInfoTheory.Certificate`;
- the finite-family certificate adapters and certificate-only example code.

General information-theory results used by certificates remain upstream. In
particular, finite-family inequalities, entropy submodularity, KL, independence,
Markov, data-processing, and sufficiency results remain LeanInfoTheory
mathematics.

`ShannonCert` must remain untouched during LeanInfoTheory release preparation.
Removing or relocating upstream ownership does not authorize modifying that
repository.

## 3. Public import and stability policy

- `LeanInfoTheory` is a lightweight, certificate-free mathematical root. Its
  exact direct imports are `LeanInfoTheory.Probability.Finite` and
  `LeanInfoTheory.InformationMeasures`.
- Focused public modules will remain available for channels, bounds, Fano,
  finite families, KL, Markov, sufficiency, convexity, and related topics.
- `LeanInfoTheory.Shannon` is the full, import-only umbrella for users who
  intentionally want the complete supported mathematical stack. It subsumes
  the lightweight root.
- Aggregator modules own no otherwise focused declarations. The semantic
  aggregate is import-only, and expected self-information is owned by
  `LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.Entropy`.
- `LeanInfoTheory.Basic`, `LeanInfoTheory.MathlibFragments`, and the
  `LeanInfoTheory.Examples` hierarchy are explicitly non-stable unless promoted
  during the API audit. Both mathematical umbrellas exclude them.
- Supported modules are expected to remain source-compatible throughout
  `0.1.x`. Intentional breaking changes require a later minor release such as
  `0.2.0` and must be documented.

The import architecture is fixed by Step 5. Step 7 freezes the exact supported
declaration and export inventory in
[`v0.1-public-api.md`](v0.1-public-api.md) and
[`v0.1-public-api.json`](v0.1-public-api.json).

## 4. Units policy

Canonical information quantities remain real-valued and measured in **nats**.
This preserves direct interoperability with mathlib's natural-log entropy and
KL APIs.

`LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.Units` will remain opt-in. It may provide a small,
explicit scalar conversion surface for arbitrary valid logarithm bases and for
bits, together with representative entropy, mutual-information,
conditional-mutual-information, and guarded real-KL examples. The project will
not create a parallel hierarchy of bit-valued definitions or duplicate the
theorem library by logarithm base.

Documentation must say "measured in nats", not "nat-valued".

Step 6 is complete locally. `Shannon.Units` remains a lightweight four-module
closure and now adds only the scalar definitions `natsToBase` and `natsToBits`;
all four pre-existing conversion theorem signatures are unchanged. The
non-stable `Examples.Units` module maintains arbitrary-base, entropy, MI, CMI,
and absolute-continuity-guarded real-KL consumers while keeping KL imports out
of the units layer. The root remains a five-module closure, and the full
Shannon umbrella remains a 31-module closure. No parallel bit-valued hierarchy,
unit-specific simplifier rule, publication action, or `ShannonCert` change is
part of this step.
