## Candidate scope

Current source contains **811 supported declarations in 57 modules**, compared
with the released 601 in 31: 210 added declarations and 26 added modules.
The 92 lightweight-root aliases and 94 reviewed simp declarations are retained.
These are inventory counts, not a measure of mathematical value or proof of
semantic compatibility. No breaking API migration is selected.

- **Quantitative probability:** half-L1 total variation, attained event and
  bounded-test forms, maximal couplings, channel/coordinate transport, Markov
  gluing, Pinsker and quantitative independence; entropy/conditional-entropy/MI
  continuity and finite-PMF topology.
- **Independent products and iid blocks:** dependent finite products, marginals,
  coordinate maps and splitting, entropy/MI and canonical KL product laws;
  product channels with arbitrary-input upper bounds and equality for independent
  inputs. Independence is explicit, never inferred from a product alphabet.
- **Finite-iid source coding:** weak AEP and strict typical sets, total
  deterministic fixed-length codes, vanishing-error achievability at every rate
  strictly above entropy, error tending to one for positive-budget families under
  an eventual rate cap strictly below entropy, and a general converse for vanishing-error
  families. The operational source threshold is an infimum statement, not a
  claim of equality-rate attainment.
- **Finite-channel capacity and converses:** attained mutual-information
  capacity on a nonempty finite input alphabet, product capacity and noiseless,
  constant, binary-symmetric and erasure-channel formulas. Finite Fano-based
  bounds and asymptotic weak converses use the same actual deterministic block
  code, uniform-message average error and rate family. Above-capacity eventual
  and infinitely-often gaps retain their distinct conclusions.

## Assumptions and limits

Information and rates use **nats**; bits require explicit scalar conversion.
The library remains finite and discrete. Capacity requires a nonempty finite
input alphabet; channel rows supply output nonemptiness. Coding statements
retain their positive-message-budget, alphabet and length hypotheses, including
their stated length-zero conventions. Total conditional laws have fallback rows
on null fibers; conditional assertions use support-sensitive assumptions.
Canonical KL is ENNReal-valued and may be infinite. Real-valued KL conversion
and product consequences retain their support/finiteness guards.

The candidate supplies no channel achievability theorem, operational channel
capacity equality, channel strong converse, continuous/differential-entropy
theory, efficient executable encoder, or full textbook/paper formalization.
The source strong converse must not be advertised as a channel strong converse.
