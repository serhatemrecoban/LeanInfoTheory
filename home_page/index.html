Project Summary

LeanInfoTheory is a Lean 4/mathlib library for finite discrete information theory. The current formalization covers entropy, conditional entropy, mutual information, conditional mutual information, independence and Markov characterizations, finite stochastic-channel data processing, deterministic-decoder Fano, dependent finite-family entropy and mutual independence, finite log-sum, entropy concavity, KL joint convexity, common-base conditional KL, MI input-law concavity and channel convexity. The goal is a reusable, proof-complete foundation that downstream Lean projects can consume through focused public imports, the lightweight LeanInfoTheory root, or the complete import-only LeanInfoTheory.Shannon umbrella.

Validation and Trust

Release declarations are checked by Lean's kernel and the maintained project builds. Continuous integration also regenerates the source-derived references and rejects proof placeholders in project Lean source.

Kernel checking establishes that formal declarations follow from their formal assumptions; theorem selection, assumptions, conventions, and mathematical interpretation remain human responsibilities.
