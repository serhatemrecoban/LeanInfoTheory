Project

A reusable Lean 4 and mathlib foundation for finite discrete information theory, designed for direct use by downstream formal developments.

Motivation

Lean already has important fragments of information theory in mathlib, including binary entropy, q-ary entropy, KL divergence, Markov-kernel infrastructure, and coding basics such as Kraft-McMillan. What is still missing is a mature information-theorist-facing layer for finite entropy, mutual information, conditional mutual information, channels, and related semantic theorems.

Current and near-term deliverables

  • A public mathlib-based Lean repository with CI and a current module guide.
  • A finite PMF-based Shannon layer with entropy, marginals, conditional entropy, mutual information, and conditional mutual information.
  • Finite entropy sanity theorems for relabeling, coordinate swaps, reassociation, the logarithmic upper bound, and the uniform-law equality case.
  • Semantic bridge theorems proving finite entropy as expected self-information over PMF.toMeasure, mutual information as KL divergence, conditional entropy as expected fiber entropy, conditional mutual information as averaged fiber mutual information and averaged fiber KL, semantic nonnegativity, and mutual-information chain rules.
  • Finite independence, conditional-independence, and Markov characterizations, including zero-MI/zero-CMI equality cases and canonical channel factorization.
  • Finite stochastic-channel data processing for mutual information and KL divergence, with deterministic, cascade, invariant-law, and doubly stochastic entropy consequences.
  • Dependent finite-family laws with finite-atom entropy, conditional entropy, mutual information, conditional mutual information, pair/triple compatibility, and ordered chain rules.
  • Finite-family Shannon inequalities, including monotonicity, submodularity, conditioning reduction, and n-way subadditivity.
  • A zero-safe finite log-sum API, finite PMF mixtures, entropy concavity, KL joint convexity with a binary equality characterization, and mutual-information input-law concavity and channel convexity.
  • Finite Fano inequalities, common-base conditional KL, sufficient-statistic recovery, and finite-family mutual-independence equality characterizations.

Current limitations

  • The semantic and Fano layers remain finite-PMF/finite-alphabet focused; general measurable extensions, exact Fano equality and sharpness, randomized/list decoding, AEP, and source/channel coding remain future work.
  • Application-specific proof automation, certificate import, and paper-specific constructions belong in downstream projects rather than this library.
  • Signature-bearing v0.1.0 API documentation is generated from the exact release commit; non-toy downstream converse examples remain separate milestones.
