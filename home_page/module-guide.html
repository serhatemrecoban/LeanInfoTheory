Imports in v0.2.0

This guide lists all 57 supported modules in v0.2.0, whose source inventory has 811 declarations. It also identifies non-stable examples and reference anchors. New modules are not part of the frozen v0.1.0 API; use its historical reference when working against that release.

Canonical information is in nats; total variation is half L1. Finite alphabets, positive-length guards, support premises and independence hypotheses remain part of each theorem's contract. The current capability map distinguishes source coding from channel weak converses.

Import LeanInfoTheory for the lightweight mathematical API, LeanInfoTheory.Shannon for the complete supported mathematical stack, or a focused module when dependency weight matters. Basic, Examples, and MathlibFragments are non-stable anchors excluded from both mathematical umbrellas.

  • LeanInfoTheory.Basic: non-stable development anchor for project metadata and roadmap vocabulary.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Probability.Finite: real-mass, finite-bind, support, and pointwise PMF.map bridge lemmas.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Probability.FiniteChannel: opt-in, type-generic PMF-valued channel constructions and their atom, projection, composition, deterministic-map, and support laws.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Probability.FiniteMixture: opt-in binary PMF mixtures with NNReal weights and exact endpoint laws; general selectors use PMF.bind.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon: import-only umbrella for the complete supported mathematical stack.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.Entropy: finite Shannon entropy, random-variable entropy, zero-entropy characterizations, and relabeling invariance theorems.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.EntropyBounds: Jensen-based alphabet and support-cardinality upper bounds plus the uniform-law equality theorem. Import this module explicitly when those heavier analytic bounds are needed.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.EntropyConcavity: opt-in finite-selector entropy concavity with a binary textbook corollary and interior-weight equality characterization.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.BinaryEntropy: opt-in Boolean-PMF bridge identifying local entropy with mathlib's Real.binEntropy.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.Continuity: opt-in exact-TV entropy continuity, a capped monotone budget bound, and conditional-entropy/MI bounds with separate whole-alphabet caps. Entropy uses nats; TV is half L1.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.Fano: opt-in deterministic-decoder error API, exact and q-ary finite Fano inequalities, weak forms, random-variable wrappers, and converse-facing corollaries.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.FiniteFamily: opt-in dependent finite-family laws, finite marginals, entropy/MI/CMI algebra, pair/triple compatibility, and binary/ordered entropy, MI, and conditional-MI chain rules.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.InfoMeasures: marginals, conditional entropy, mutual information, conditional mutual information, chain rules, symmetry, and textbook entropy identities.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.LogSum: opt-in zero-safe finite EReal log-sum inequality and equality characterization with guarded Real corollaries.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.Units: lightweight opt-in scalar conversion from canonical nats to arbitrary valid logarithm bases, with base 2 named for bits. It remains outside the lightweight root and does not duplicate the information-measure hierarchy.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge: import-only bridge entry point for expected self-information, finite conditional laws, KL and conditional-KL formulas, convexity, independence, Markov and sufficiency semantics, recovery, stochastic data processing, pair/triple inequalities, Pinsker and quantitative independence, and finite-family semantics.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.Entropy: focused owner for self-information and finite entropy as expected self-information.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.Product: independent-product PMF infrastructure, product-measure bridge lemmas, and joint-law absolute-continuity facts.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.FiniteSums: finite real-sum rewrites for marginals, entropy, and the log-ratio formula for mutual information.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.Conditional: finite conditional-law API and expected conditional entropy / conditional mutual information fiber formulas.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.ConditionalKL: common-base channel conditional KL, finite weighted semantics, the joint KL chain rule, and support-guarded Real forms.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.Convexity: finite-selector KL joint convexity, binary KL equality, and mutual-information input-law concavity and channel convexity.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.FiniteFamily: finite-family monotonicity, nonnegativity, submodularity, MI bounds, conditioning reduction, and binary/n-way subadditivity.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.FiniteFamilyIndependence: finite-atom PMF/source mutual independence, restriction and pair laws, and n-way entropy-additivity equality characterizations.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.KL: finite KL expansion, mutual information as KL, and averaged conditional-KL bridge theorems.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.Independence: ordinary and conditional independence predicates, mathlib bridges, positive-fiber formulations, and zero-MI/zero-CMI characterizations.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.Markov: finite Markov predicates and characterizations, total conditional channels, canonical/existential channel factorization, exact information loss, and MI data processing.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.Sufficiency: lightweight fixed-prior and model-family sufficiency predicates, information-preservation characterizations, exact full-joint recovery, and finite factorization results.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.DataProcessing: PMF-channel-to-kernel and posterior bridges, exact KL decomposition, finite KL contraction, invariant-law consequences, and doubly stochastic entropy growth.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.Pinsker: canonical finite-PMF Pinsker in nats, support-guarded Real and square-root bounds, and mutual-information bounds on distance from a joint law to its marginal product.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.Sufficiency.KL: downstream KL preservation and guarded converse theorems for sufficient channels and deterministic statistics.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.Theorems: user-facing semantic theorems, including nonnegativity, zero conditional entropy, deterministic entropy/MI processing, chain-rule variants, and ordinary/conditional inequality bands.
  • LeanInfoTheory.InformationMeasures: public convenience re-export for the finite information-measure API; theorem-oriented code should usually prefer the canonical LeanInfoTheory.Shannon names.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Probability.TotalVariation: finite-PMF total variation as half L1, with event and bounded-test characterizations, transport and contraction.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Probability.Coupling: finite couplings and an attained maximal coupling whose disagreement probability is total variation.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Probability.FiniteTopology: finite-PMF topology and compactness, including boundary probability laws.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Probability.TotalVariationTopology: total-variation convergence and the finite-PMF topology.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Probability.FiniteProduct: dependent finite-product laws, marginals and selected-coordinate transport with the required index hypotheses.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Probability.Iid: finite iid word laws, including zero-length words and finite-block transport.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Probability.ProductChannel: product and repeated PMF-valued channels with actual input/output joint laws and coordinate projections.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Probability.StandardChannels: finite deterministic and input-independent channels, binary symmetric channels and binary erasure channels.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.Topology: continuity of finite entropy, conditional entropy and mutual information, including the actual channel-input objective.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.Gluing: support-local extension and adjacent-law gluing; the resulting endpoint coupling need not be maximal.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.FiniteProduct: finite-product entropy and independence identities.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.ProductKL: finite-product KL decomposition with extended-valued and guarded real forms.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.ProductChannel: product-channel information bounds for actual laws, including correlated inputs and independence-qualified equalities.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SemanticBridge.AEP: supported block self-information and finite iid concentration/AEP through finite-moment and Chebyshev bounds.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.TypicalSet: strict supported weak typical sets, with probability, atom-mass and cardinality estimates and explicit boundary cases.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.CodingRate: natural message budgets, normalized rates, integer rounding and binary padding for finite fixed-length coding.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SourceCoding: total deterministic finite source codes, actual error, selected-set recovery, success counting and Fano bounds.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.SourceCodingAsymptotic: one-family above-entropy achievability, source strong converse, necessary rates for arbitrary reliable families and the entropy threshold as an IsGLB; no efficient encoder or exact-threshold attainment claim.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.ChannelCapacity: finite information capacity with an actual maximizing input law for a nonempty finite input alphabet.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.ProductChannelCapacity: attained product and repeated information capacities, using an upper bound valid for correlated block inputs.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.StandardChannelCapacity: attained noiseless, input-independent, binary symmetric and binary erasure information capacities, including endpoint parameters and explicit bits conversions.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.ChannelCode: deterministic finite channel codes, uniform message laws, encoder pushforwards and actual decoding error; encoder collisions are permitted.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.ChannelCoding: same-code Fano, data-processing and single-letter information-capacity bounds, with all-length and guarded normalized weak-converse forms.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Shannon.ChannelCodingAsymptotic: arbitrary-family weak converses with derived boundedness for real limsup, distinguishing eventual error floors from frequent-gap nonvanishing.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Examples.SupportSensitive: opt-in support-cardinality, conditional-fiber, functional-dependence, and recovery examples.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Examples.KLTop: opt-in disjoint-support example demonstrating the need for finiteness guards in real-valued KL equality statements.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Examples.CommonCause: opt-in noisy binary common-cause example exercising conditional independence, zero CMI, and Markov factorization.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Examples.ConditionalKL: opt-in private regression consumers for null, active, finite, infinite, and support-guarded common-base conditional KL.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Examples.StochasticChannels: opt-in stochastic reset/cascade examples exercising KL contraction, support-sensitive real bounds, and strict entropy growth.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Examples.SufficientStatistics: opt-in fixed-prior and family-sufficiency examples covering recovery, factorization, KL preservation, and failure modes.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Examples.Convexity: opt-in private regression consumers for scalar log-sum, finite mixtures, entropy concavity, KL convexity, and MI input-law concavity and channel convexity.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Examples.Fano: opt-in examples covering perfect decoding, the singleton-source edge case, and a fair Boolean source with a constant observation.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Examples.FiniteFamily: opt-in homogeneous and dependent-alphabet examples exercising ordered entropy/MI/CMI chains, overlap, empty entropy, product laws, and pairwise-but-not-mutual independence.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Examples.Units: opt-in maintained consumers for arbitrary-base, bits, entropy, MI, CMI, and finiteness-guarded real-KL conversion.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Examples.Pinsker: private endpoint, tied-event, sparse finite-KL, budget and singular-support regression consumers.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Examples.QuantitativeIndependence: private MI/event/test budgets, arbitrary-source random variables and sparse joint-law regressions.
  • LeanInfoTheory.Examples: non-stable aggregate for all maintained regression consumers, including information-unit conversions.
  • LeanInfoTheory.MathlibFragments: non-stable reference anchor for heavier mathlib information-theory and coding imports.
