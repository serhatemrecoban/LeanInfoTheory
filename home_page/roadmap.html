This roadmap separates completed Lean/site work from active theorem planning, planned theorem work, and later infrastructure milestones. The labels are intentionally conservative: they describe the current repository, not aspirations.

Second release

Current source has 811 supported declarations across 57 modules and retains 92 lightweight-root exports. The finite programme now includes total variation/coupling, Pinsker and continuity, finite products and topology, iid AEP, fixed-length source coding, attained information capacity and actual-code channel weak converses. See the capability boundaries. The versioned API and downstream case study provide practical entry points.

Implemented mathematical coverage

  • Finite entropy, conditional entropy, mutual information and conditional mutual information, with chain rules and support-sensitive equality cases.
  • Independence and Markov characterizations, stochastic data processing, finite sufficiency, Fano inequalities, finite-family identities, convexity and conditional KL.
  • Total variation, maximal coupling, transport and gluing, Pinsker bounds, quantitative independence, continuity and finite-PMF topology.
  • Dependent finite products, iid blocks, product entropy and KL, and product-channel mutual information with arbitrary-input upper bounds.
  • Finite-iid AEP, strict typical sets, deterministic fixed-length source codes, above-entropy achievability and source-coding converses.
  • Attained finite-channel information capacity, standard channel formulas, and actual-code finite and asymptotic channel weak converses.

See the capability map for hypotheses and limitations, and the module guide for focused imports.

Released foundation

v0.1.0 remains the historical foundation, with its original versioned API documentation, GitHub Release and Zenodo archive. The Perfect Functional Representations case study demonstrates reuse of that unchanged foundation.
