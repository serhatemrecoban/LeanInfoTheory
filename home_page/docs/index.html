Examples from the third release

  • Finite hypothesis testing gives a most powerful randomized test at a prescribed size.
  • Information density and one-shot coding bound the decoding error at a chosen blocklength and message count.
  • Operational capacity and expurgation connect achievable rates to information capacity and control the worst retained message error.
  • Lossless source–channel separation gives reliable reconstruction when entropy is strictly below capacity.
  • Binary prefix coding bounds the expected number of bits per iid source symbol.
  • The method of types computes the probability of a whole empirical class, including impossible classes.
